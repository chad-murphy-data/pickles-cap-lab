How it works

The ratings behind the prices

Each price starts with a $45,000 baseline for a roster spot. Performance value and a singles adjustment make up the rest. Players with the same full rating profile within a gender get the same price. Confidence is shown separately.

Singles ranks include only players with at least one recorded, non-forfeit 2026 singles game through October 7. We rank those players within gender by estimated singles ability. Players without a recent singles game show “Not ranked.” Their projected ability still contributes to DreamBreakers, using the available historical results, DreamBreaker rallies and doubles-informed estimates. Withholding a rank does not change a player’s price or playing estimate.

Ratings and prices are fixed as of October 7, 2026. The full price book includes 2,208 players with recorded 2026 activity. You can choose from the top 100 doubles-rated players of each gender here. Being listed doesn’t confirm a player’s 2027 eligibility or availability.

What your team score means

Your score runs from 0 to 100. The model compares each team against the same six opponent profiles, including doubles, mixed doubles and DreamBreakers. We scale that average match-win estimate between two legal benchmark teams: the weakest and strongest found in a repeatable search of this player pool.

Both benchmarks follow the $500k–$1M budget and three-of-each-gender rules. A score of 50 is halfway between their modeled strength. It isn’t a 50% win chance or a player percentile. The search does not prove global limits; teams beyond either benchmark display 0 or 100.

The example teams are built separately, so they can share players. In the draft, each player can join only one team.

Lineups, mixed doubles and DreamBreakers

The model selects the two highest doubles-rated players of each gender for doubles. It chooses mixed pairs by combined modeled pair strength and averages the two possible opposing mixed-pair orders. This gives a suggested lineup; it doesn’t test every possible tactical choice.

Matches include women’s doubles, men’s doubles and two mixed-doubles games, modeled as races to 11, win by two. A 2–2 split leads to a DreamBreaker. The best two singles profiles per gender play in M–M–F–F order, four rallies each, to 21, win by two. All six roster members may therefore have a role. For this game, reserves can take part in the DreamBreaker.

The model uses a coefficient of 0.42 to convert singles-rating differences into DreamBreaker rally chances. That conversion still needs more validation against real matches. Men’s and women’s ratings are separate scales. Mixed doubles combines them using the PICKLES doubles convention.

How the prices were set

The model estimates what each player adds compared with players below the top 60 of the same gender, using shared team contexts. It uses one dollar scale for both genders. The largest measured contribution becomes $730,000; the $45,000 baseline puts Waters at $775,000 and leaves room for five minimum-price players. Prices round down in $5,000 steps. This is a chosen scale for the roster budget, not an estimate of what a market would pay.

The singles adjustment accounts for singles ability above or below what the model expects at that doubles level. Performance value already includes that expected singles contribution. Select a player’s name to see the breakdown.

What we checked, and what’s still uncertain

In three drafts with the 20 most expensive players forced into round one, 19 of 20 teams in every run finished between 45% and 55% modeled round-robin win rates. Waters’ team was the exception above 55%. Unseeded drafts sometimes left Fahey and Todd undrafted. These seeded drafts show that competitive teams can be built at these prices. They don’t show that owners would choose to pay those prices.

The checks verify the calculations and draft rules. They don’t establish how accurately the model predicts real matches. It doesn’t directly account for chemistry, injuries, availability, tactical matchups or fatigue. Small differences in the results deserve some caution.
